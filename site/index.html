Exhibit B: How it works
It plays itself billions of times. After every decision it asks how much better
each other action would have done, and leans toward the ones it regrets not taking.
The average of all that play is the blueprint. At the table, on the turn and river,
it reads each range from the betting and re-solves the spot a few actions deep,
letting every opponent switch style at the horizon. Then it plays.
