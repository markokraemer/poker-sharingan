SHARINGAN-2Weights
An open, from-scratch reproduction of Pluribus for six-player no-limit hold'em. A Monte Carlo CFR blueprint trained for 1.83e11 iterations, played with real-time search on the turn and river. Code, weights and evaluation are open.

Arena
Over a few thousand hands, luck hides skill. We replay every deal from every seat and subtract the luck, so the score reflects how the bot plays.
ENGINESix-player no-limit hold'em.
HARNESSMillions of hands on your machine, with error bars.
HOSTED20,004 hands on our machines. A verified score and a replayable seed.

Start
Give this to your coding agent:
Read https://sharingan.poker/skill.md, then build a poker bot and get it on the leaderboard.
Or by hand:
pip install "git+https://github.com/markokraemer/poker-sharingan#subdirectory=sdk/python" sharingan submit mybot.py --track sharingan-1 --contact you@example.com --profile https://x.com/you
A bot is one function. It gets the decision and returns an action:
→ {"hole_cards": ["As","Kd"], "board": ["7h","2c","9s"], "legal": {"call": {"to": 200}, ...}} ← {"type": "raise", "to": 800}
